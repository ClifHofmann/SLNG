# v0.28.46-alpha — Preferences Reworked: Seven Categories and a Search

Since v0.28.40-alpha the Preferences window is rebuilt: seven categories instead of twelve tabs, one layout for every setting, expert options folded away per section, and a search field that filters the window as you type, the way Firestorm's does. Three settings that could change by themselves are fixed along the way.

## Preferences, reorganised (FEAT-UI-79)

- **Seven categories instead of twelve tabs:** General, Graphics, Camera & movement, Chat & IM, Keyboard, Network & cache, Help & info. Each is one scrolling column of sections; settings that used to sit on a catch-all "Display" page are now where you would look for them (names above avatars under General, friends' logon notices under Chat & IM).
- **Graphics has no sub-tabs any more.** A header with the quality slider and a **Profiles** menu (click a saved profile to load it, "Save current as…", a Delete submenu), then View & detail, Shadows, Light & effects, Depth of field and Performance.
- **"Advanced" per section.** Options a newcomer rarely needs (shadow cascades, SSIL and SSR, the render thread, texture compression, the chat-log file names, the camera pad speeds …) sit in a collapsed **Advanced** fold-out at the end of their section. Whether you opened it is remembered, as is the category you had open.
- **Greyed out, not hidden.** A setting that only works while another one is on (the shadow sliders, the depth-of-field controls, the real-time mirror probe, the manual UI scale) is dimmed while its switch is off, so you see what the switch unlocks and nothing jumps around.
- **One mirror switch.** "Mirrors" is the main switch; the real-time mirror probe that needs it is under Light & effects › Advanced.
- **A badge says when a change applies** if it is not at once: *Restart* (render thread), *Next login* (chat-log folder), *Reopen windows* (language).
- The top menu's Graphics entry opens Preferences on Graphics. The window opens at 760 × 520 and fits down to 640 × 420.

## Search in Preferences (FEAT-UI-80)

- **A search field above the categories** filters on every keystroke: categories and sections without a hit disappear, hits are highlighted and the first one is scrolled into view. Esc clears the field; clearing brings everything back.
- **It looks inside Advanced.** A hit in a folded Advanced opens it while you search and folds it back afterwards. A hit on a greyed-out setting highlights the switch that unlocks it too.
- **German, English and Second Life names.** Every setting is found by its text in your language and in English, and by the reference viewer's setting name where one exists, so `RenderFarClip`, `MaxFPS` or "bandwidth" work in the German UI. Accents do not matter, and several words must all occur.
- **Keyboard shortcuts take part.** A term that matches a key action keeps the Keyboard category in the list and fills its own filter.

## Fixed

- **Opening Preferences could change settings.** Redrawing the Graphics page wrote values back: it could switch depth-of-field auto-focus off and round shadow and depth-of-field sliders to their step. Nothing is written any more when the window opens, redraws or closes.
- **Texture memory "Automatic" overwrote your manual size.** Switching to Automatic stored the automatic budget as the manual value, so switching back lost what you had set. The manual size now stays.
- **The mouse wheel over a slider changed the setting** instead of scrolling the page, easy to do by accident in the longer columns. It now scrolls.
- **Typing indicators no longer fill the console.** Each time someone typed in an IM, three lines went to the console and `godot.log`. They now appear only with `--diag` or in the debug log.

## Not yet confirmed in-world

- The window and the search had a first look in-world. The Profiles menu round trip (save, load, delete) and the window in the English UI are covered by the self-test only.

## Still missing

- More informational lines (cache, region, appearance and memory reports) still print without `--diag`; moving them to the debug log is under way (BUG-UI-42).
- The experimental separate render thread can freeze the viewer on NVIDIA cards (seen twice). Keep Graphics › Performance › Advanced › Render thread on "Main thread".
- A landmark list with online status and an "offline" filter (FEAT-UI-78).
- Hundreds of `Leaked unsafe reference` lines for shader materials can appear when the viewer quits after a long session; the cause is not found yet.
