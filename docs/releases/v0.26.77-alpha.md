# v0.26.77-alpha — A friends list you can sort and read at a glance, a chat window that tells you when something is waiting, and HUD editing

Since v0.26.60 the Friends tab has your own categories, rights columns like the reference viewer's, and a view you can narrow; the communication window's tabs are easier to hit and the Chat tab shows unread messages; worn HUDs can be selected and edited on the HUD plane; and two chat-focus and notification bugs are fixed.

## Friends list

- **Your own categories.** The **+** next to the filter makes one; right-click a friend (or the *Category…* button) to file them, *New category…* in that menu files them into it at once. A category is a header with a fold arrow, its name and `online/total`; click it to fold or open it. Friends in none come last under *No category*. Right-click a header to rename, delete (it asks; the friends stay on the list) or move it up or down. Until the first category exists the list looks as before.
- **Reorder by dragging.** Each header has a six-dot grip; drag it onto another header and it takes that header's place. A bar on the target's top or bottom edge shows where it will land; a drop on *No category* means last.
- **Show only online** and **Show categories** checkboxes. Only online hides offline friends and the categories nobody in is online; Show categories off gives one plain list and keeps your filing. Both are remembered per account, like the categories themselves (stored locally; the grid has no such thing).
- **Rights columns, as in the reference viewer.** Under *Friend may…* three icons you click: see me online (eye), see me on the map (pin), edit my objects (pencil). Under *I may…* two icons that only the friend can change: I see them on the map, I may edit their objects. A right that is on is drawn in colour (blue = what you grant, green = what the friend granted), one that is off as a faint ghost. **Granting *edit* asks first**; taking it back does not. The change goes to the grid and survives a relog. When a friend gives you a right or takes one back, a notification says so.
- **The login name stands behind the Display Name** (muted, on the same line) instead of under it, and the online dot is a proper 14 px disc with an Online / Offline tooltip.

## Communication window

- **The whole tab is clickable** — icon, name and the padding around them, with a hover tint — not only its name.
- **The Chat tab shows unread messages** with the same red count as the toolbar's chat button, and turns warm, while another tab such as Friends is in front. A message for the conversation you have selected now counts while you are on another tab, and clears when you come back.
- **The chat bar no longer goes dead after Enter.** Godot ends a text field's editing state when Enter submits it, so the cursor stayed but typing did nothing until you clicked in again. It also affected the World Map's search field, now fixed too.
- **Friend logs in or out:** a pop-up at the top right (Preferences → Display switches it off); the icon-bar chat button breathes while messages are waiting. The pop-up shows the person's Display Name, and waits a moment for it, instead of "Someone is online." right after login.
- The chat log keeps following new lines when the typing line changes its height; an IM opened by a typing indicator gets the speaker's name; a Second Life group chat is titled with the group's name and a muted group stays muted; a group **notice** is a notification, never a chat tab; "Hide my group title" now applies after every login.

## HUDs

Worn HUDs can be selected like objects (right-click → Edit / Detach, pick in build mode), and moved, stretched and turned with the handles **on the HUD plane**; stretching keeps the textures while you drag.

## Not yet confirmed in-world

Built, tested and read, but not yet seen on a grid: the rights icons and their notifications against a second account (the direction of what you grant versus what the friend granted is pinned by a test, but please check it once), the drag grip and drop bar, clicking the tabs and the Chat tab's unread count, the chat bar keeping its focus after Enter, the friend pop-up's names, and the HUD handles.

## Still missing

Voice, world sound, a single "n friends are online" pop-up at login (each friend still gets their own), unread counting for a closed window's selected conversation, leaving a conference session on the grid, land buying, and the Second Life shortcuts listed in the manual. Alpha: log in with an account you could afford to lose.
