# v0.28.40-alpha — Favorites Bar from the Inventory, Offline Regions Shown, Quieter Console

Since v0.28.24-alpha the favorites bar shows the Favorites folder of your inventory, the login screen's "Start at" list follows it, a favorite whose region is down is marked before you teleport, and refused teleports say why in plain words. The console is far quieter.

## Favorites

- **The favorites bar is your inventory's Favorites folder (FEAT-UI-72).** The same places Firestorm shows. The old list that only this viewer kept is gone and was not migrated.
  - Click a place to teleport. Right-click: teleport, copy SLurl, remove from favorites (the copy goes to the Trash, the original landmark stays).
  - **Add:** drag a landmark from the inventory or the landmarks window onto the bar, or use **+** > add current location. The landmark is copied into the folder. While you drag, a card at the pointer says what dropping does.
  - Favorites added or removed in another viewer appear after a moment. The order is kept by this viewer; a new favorite goes first.
- **The login list follows the bar.** A favorite added or removed during a session reaches the login screen's "Start at" list for the next login, also when it was added shortly before logging out (the logout waits up to 8 seconds to look it up). Landmarks are fetched over the ViewerAsset capability first; the older transfer is the fallback.
- **A favorite whose region is down is marked (FEAT-UI-77).** In the bar it shows a warning sign, dimmed, with a tooltip; it stays clickable because the region may be back. On the login screen it is a greyed "(region offline)" line, because nothing says which region it points at. What the last session found is shown at once; it is checked again at login.
- **Optional: check the regions regularly.** Preferences > Network has a checkbox, off by default. On, every 5 minutes the viewer asks the grid once per favorite region, so a region that goes down or comes back shows in the bar. Switching it on checks at once.

## Teleports and login

- **Refused teleports say why.** A refusal came as the grid's internal code (`no_host`, `noaccess_tport`). It now reads as the reference viewer's sentence in your language: destination not found, no access to the destination, teleport limit reached, and so on (15 cases).
- **A login that landed elsewhere says so, twice.** The notice now says the place could not be reached (its region may be offline, or you have no access). If the one teleport the viewer then tries is refused, that message adds that you were redirected and why you stay where you are.
- **A silent region is named (BUG-NET-36).** When the region never answers over UDP, the viewer says so at once and points at the firewall, instead of leaving you with sky and water.

## Camera

- **Zoom into a person (FEAT-UI-73).** A new Camera preference, "Disable camera constraints" (off by default), lets the wheel zoom go all the way into an avatar like Firestorm. The Alt+Click focus point now sits on the skin instead of a hand's width in front of it.

## Console and logs

- **The console and `godot.log` are quiet while the debug log is on.** The detailed per-object diagnostics the debug log switches on go to its file alone; only `--diag` also prints them. The same holds for the library's warnings (errors still print), refused-texture notes, the region-load summary and the list of attachments the viewer puts back on at login.
- Not changed: the `p_image` error at start-up is a Godot engine bug with the font cache on the separate render thread. It is harmless and cannot be silenced from here.

## Not yet confirmed in-world

- The region-silent message (BUG-NET-36) needs a blocked UDP connection to see.
- Group notices setting after a restart.
