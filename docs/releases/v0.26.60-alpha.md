# v0.26.60-alpha — Faster with a mesh body, a performance overlay that says where the frame goes, and a better chat window

Since v0.26.28 a heavily dressed avatar no longer costs a fifth of the frame, the performance overlay splits every frame into its parts, the open radar no longer drags the frame rate down, and the chat window gained a Recent tab, conference chats, typing indicators, display names and profile pictures.

## Faster with a mesh body

- **Fully transparent worn meshes are no longer drawn.** Mesh bodies hide their unused layers (tattoo, underwear, clothing) and the segments an outfit cuts away by setting those faces to alpha 0. SLNG drew nothing for such a face, but still spent a draw call on it in every pass (depth, colour and each shadow cascade) and skinned it on the GPU every frame. A worn rigged mesh none of whose faces shows anything is now hidden completely, as the reference viewer does, and is also detached from the skeleton so its bones are no longer updated. A layer switched on from the body HUD brings it back.
- **Measured on one spot, alone, same avatar:** 49 → 60-62 fps. The avatar had 255 skinned meshes, of which 52 actually show anything; 680 of its 743 faces were alpha 0. With another, lighter avatar the same spot ran at 70 fps before the fix, which is how the avatar was found.
- Nothing looks different: only instances that already drew nothing are hidden. Transparent rigid attachments are left alone, because they are often click targets.

## The performance overlay says where the frame goes

`Ctrl+Shift+1` (and the `[Perf]` line in `slng-perf.log`) now answer "CPU or GPU, and which part":

- **CPU** row: `scripts · flush · draw · other` — every `_Process` callback, the deferred work (skeletons, redraws, transforms), the whole RenderingServer draw, and physics/input. They add up to the frame.
- **Render** row: Godot's own render time on the CPU and on the GPU; **Views** row: the same per view (main, HUD, mirror).
- **Draw** row: draw calls split into scene and shadow passes.
- `[PhaseCost]` covers the code that had no measurement before (texture animation, environment, UI, particles and more), and an `[AvatarCost]` line counts skinned meshes, hidden faces and skin binds.
- The old "process" figure was Godot's whole process step including rendering, and a worst case — never the time of our scripts. It is still logged as `processMs`, but the overlay no longer shows it.

## The radar no longer costs frame rate

With the radar open the frame rate fell from over 50 to about 22. The radar scanned every entity of the region each frame, asked for unknown names every frame and drew two rectangles per listed prim. It now keeps its avatar list and refreshes it twice a second, updates at most 30 times a second, asks for a name at most every 5 s, and draws the object layer as one picture that is repainted only when the object list changes.

## Conversations

- **Recent tab:** the last 30 IM and group conversations, newest first, with the last line and the time. A click reopens the conversation with its history; the logs on disk are never touched.
- **Conference chats** (ad-hoc group IMs) arrived as group chat, in a tab titled with a UUID, with "typing" lines and every own line twice. They now have their own tab and replies go back into the session; *Show conference chats as IMs* (Preferences → Display) files them as plain IMs instead, like Firestorm.
- **Typing indicator:** "Name is typing…" under an open IM and a small … in the list; the conversation opens as soon as somebody starts typing, as in Firestorm.
- **Display names** in the chat, the Friends list and Recent; logs keep the login name so Firestorm still finds them.
- **Profile pictures:** a small picture before every IM name, in Recent and in the Friends list (a generic person symbol when there is none). The name list can be dragged wider.
- **Sort by hand:** drag a conversation in the name list to move it (for the session).
- **Presence:** the online dot in the chat follows friends logging in and out, and an open conversation says "Name is online." / "Name is offline."
- **Group chat tabs** no longer keep the group's UUID as their title.
- **Friendship offers:** write your own text before sending; a friend you just added now gets their online status asked for.

## Camera

The mouse wheel zooms in eased steps towards the point under the cursor without turning the camera, and Alt+Click picks a person by their skeleton instead of the grass behind them, focusing on their surface.

## Groups

The Groups list has toggles for receiving group chat, receiving notices and listing the group in your profile.

## Avatars and animations

- **Animations have their own download lane** and a set loads in parallel, so on a busy region poses no longer wait behind hundreds of textures (and no longer time out before they were even requested).
- **A pose switched on furniture now reaches the other avatar too:** a refusal from the region is believed for 5 s at first instead of 10 minutes, an incomplete set is asked for again, and the newest request wins over a slower older one.

## Fixes

- **"Stack overflow." after login** when the seed capability failed: LibreMetaverse retried endlessly and recursively; the loop is now stopped.

## Not yet confirmed in-world

- **Switching a mesh-body layer on from its HUD** after the transparent-mesh change: it must appear at once and move with the body.
- Conference chats, the typing indicator, the online status of a new friend, poses on furniture for the other avatar, and the camera pick against a real posed avatar have been checked by tests and reading, not on a grid.

## Still missing

Leaving a group from the list, the Access tab of About Land and the Region / Estate windows, land buying, voice, sound, group **notices**, friend **rights**, leaving a conference session on the grid (closing its tab is local), and the Second Life shortcuts listed in the manual. Alpha: log in with an account you could afford to lose.

## Verification

Both projects build, **2,659 tests green** (up from 2,569 in v0.26.28), `dotnet format` clean, shader globals 30/30. The engine selftest was not run for this release (no Godot in the build environment); the release workflow builds and tests the release configuration. The frame-rate figures above were measured in-world on the release candidate.
