# v0.28.24-alpha — Away & Auto-response, Debug Log, Image Viewer, Better Menus

Since v0.28.3-alpha you can go Away or switch on Do Not Disturb and let the viewer answer instant messages for you, open textures and profile pictures in an image viewer, reach your own profile from a right-click on your avatar, and switch on a debug log that is safe to send in. Offline instant messages now arrive at login with their real send time.

## Chat and status

- **Away, Do Not Disturb and automatic answers (FEAT-UI-75).** Modelled on Firestorm. The Avatar menu has four switches: Away, Do Not Disturb, Autorespond and Autorespond to non-friends. While you are Away or busy the top bar says so; clicking it makes you available again.
  - **Away:** sets the away flag and animation on the grid, so other viewers show "(Away)". The viewer goes Away by itself after a quiet spell (default 5 minutes, from 2 minutes to 2 hours, or never) and comes back with the next key press or click, at the earliest ten seconds after going Away. Going Away when the viewer window loses focus is a separate switch, off by default: looking at another window is not being away.
  - **Answers:** only the first live message of a conversation is answered, once, and never a blocked avatar, a conversation you started or a message the grid stored while you were offline. Do Not Disturb wins over Autorespond to non-friends, which wins over Autorespond; Away answers only if you tick "Answer instant messages while Away".
  - **Texts:** four response texts, a timeout and the switches live in Preferences > Away & Auto-response. Switches act at once; Apply and Revert are only enabled while a text has been edited. An emptied text falls back to the default.
  - The answer you sent appears in the conversation tab, and another viewer's automatic answer is now shown to you instead of being dropped.
- **Offline instant messages (BUG-NET-35).** Messages the grid kept while you were away are fetched at login, and they carry the time they were sent instead of the time you logged in.

## Debug log

- **A debug log for bug reports (FEAT-UI-76).** Preferences > Debug log has one checkbox. While it is on, everything the viewer prints, including the detailed diagnostics that are normally off, is written to its own file. Every line is cleaned first:
  - IDs, avatar names and region names become short tags. The same tag means the same thing inside one file, but it cannot be turned back into the value and does not match the next session's.
  - Passwords, tokens, session ids, IP addresses, e-mail addresses, the path part of web addresses and your Windows user name are removed.
  - Chat and instant message text is never logged.
  - Text typed into the world, such as object or parcel names, cannot be recognised, so read the file before you share it.
- **Finding the file:** the tab shows the folder and has buttons to open it, to show the current file in the file manager and to copy the path. The last ten sessions are kept; one file stops at 64 MB. Logging slows the viewer down a little.

## Interface

- **Image viewer.** Double-click a texture in the inventory, or click a picture in a profile, to open it. The window opens at the picture's own size (smaller only if it would not fit the screen). "Free transform" stretches the picture to the window's shape; off, it keeps its proportions. A second click on the same picture raises the open window.
- **Profile pictures are sharp.** They are loaded at full resolution instead of the blocky copy kept for the world, and are larger.
- **Right-click your avatar for your profile.** Most of an avatar's body is worn mesh, so a right-click gave the object menu (Edit, Touch, Inspect, Detach) and no way to your profile. A worn item's menu now offers its wearer's Profile above its own entries.
- **Nametags.** Right-click a nametag for the same menu as the body, double-click it to open the profile.
- **Script dialog buttons have one width.** A menu from a piece of furniture showed every button at the width of its own text. They now share the width of the longest label, a long label is shortened with "…" and shows its full text as a tooltip, and the window grows to fit.
- **Window title bar.** The minimise and close symbols are drawn instead of typed, and the bar is slimmer.
- **Compact Camera Controls (FEAT-UI-74).** The window is smaller and no longer chunky: an icon row on top, smaller pads, and it scales to 2x.
- **Group notices setting is sent reliably.** A lost network packet could drop the change, and the next refresh showed the old value.

## Not yet confirmed in-world

- Autoresponse: the answer to a first message with Do Not Disturb, Autorespond and Autorespond to non-friends, and its text appearing in the conversation.
- Going Away by itself after the timeout, and coming back on input.
- The debug log with a real session: the file's contents and the three buttons.
- Right-click on a worn item showing the Profile entry on another person's avatar (only the menu on your own is confirmed).

## Still missing

- **Do Not Disturb** only answers; it does not silence notifications or decline offers as the reference viewer does.
- The settings of Away and the answers are global, not per account, and Away and Do Not Disturb do not survive a restart.
- No "(Away)" tag on your own nametag.
- An inventory item attached to an answer, and per-friend answers.
- Preferences tabs do not all follow one rule yet for when a change takes effect.
